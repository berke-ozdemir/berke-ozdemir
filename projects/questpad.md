# QuestPad

A productivity app for turning goals into plans and focusing on the next task.

**Status:** Public website and login; development paused · **Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase · **Source:** Private

[Website](https://questpad.app) · [App](https://app.questpad.app)

## The project

QuestPad combines goal planning with a focused task workflow. The product includes an application, a separate marketing site, and writing about goals and focused work.

The project has evolved through different positioning and interface iterations, including quest-themed productivity and a focus on making the next action clear.

## Areas of work

- Goal planning and task-focused interfaces.
- Authentication and data storage with Supabase.
- Application and landing-page development.
- Product positioning, onboarding, and supporting content.

The website and app login are reachable. Development is paused, and remaining launch-hardening work should not be read as complete simply because the endpoints are online.
