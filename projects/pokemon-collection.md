# Pokémon collection app

A mobile-first app for arranging Pokémon cards into personal binders and sharing collection layouts.

**Status:** Pre-release · **Stack:** React, TypeScript, Supabase, Capacitor · **Source:** Private

## The project

The core experience is a creative binder editor. A new visitor can start with an empty binder and explore layouts without first claiming to own cards. Catalog selections, owned-card records, and presentation are treated as distinct concepts.

The project also explores the harder parts of a collection app: persistence, recovering drafts, moving between guest and account states, importing and exporting data, and keeping changes coherent across views.

## Areas of work

- Binder and page composition for owned or imagined collections.
- Local persistence, draft recovery, and data portability.
- Explicit handling of ownership, valuation, and authenticity claims.
- Account, sharing, and collector-community features in development.
- Web and native packaging through Capacitor.

The local editor and integration work are further along than the hosted services. Public web availability and App Store / Google Play releases have not been established. Pokémon names and materials belong to their respective rights holders.
